Kinetic Layers · design plan · Sept 2026

A light room with one warm lamp, and everything in it stacked in layers.

This is the full design direction for kineticlayers.com before we build the rest of the pages: brand idea, logo system, colour, glow, type, patterns, motion, components and the page map. Nothing below is final until you say so.

01

The idea in one line

Every asset is a stack of layers you can peel back — output, source, the site it shipped on. The brand shows that stacking everywhere: in the logo, the cards, the way sections move.

KINETIC Things move because you moved. Scroll separates layers, the cursor bends the glow, hover lifts a card. Nothing loops for decoration.
LAYERS Nested, offset, translucent. Cards sit on cards. The logo is four corners inside each other. Depth comes from stacking, not from shadow.
THE LAMP One warm light source on a near-white room. It marks what matters: the hero object, the featured plan, the closing ask. Never more than one lamp per screen.
02

Logo system

Your reference is a nested corner with a soft gradient and one rounded foot. It already says "layers", so we keep the construction and turn it into a family: same three nested corners, different fills for different jobs.

Mono — the mark Graphite to stone gradient, your reference almost exactly. Favicon, app icon, footer, anywhere it has to sit quietly.
Lit — the hero mark Same shape, the lamp catches the inner corner. Used once per page at most: hero, upgrade card, share image.
Line — the system icon Hairline version. Becomes the grammar for the whole icon set: shelves, drops, source, license all drawn as nested corners.
CONSTRUCTION RULES
Three corners, each inset by a quarter of the previous. Never two, never four. Only the outer bottom-left corner is rounded (radius = 25% of side). The others stay sharp so the eye reads a stack, not a bubble. Gradient runs top-left dark to bottom-right light. The light always comes from where the corners open. Grain is allowed on the mark at large sizes (like your reference); dropped below 32px.
WORDMARK
Kinetic Layers
Set in the body face at weight 400, tracking −3%. Two words, no camel case, no stacking the words. The mark sits left, cap-height tall.
03

Colour

A warm-white room, graphite ink, one amber lamp. Then a single cool counterweight so the amber has something to be warm against. That is the whole palette; everything else is a tint of these.

ASSET TILE TINTS
Six paper tints for previews when there's no image yet. Warm for Craft, cool for Build, moss-grey for Agents, so the shelf reads before the label does.
RULES
Amber is never a fill for large surfaces. It is light, a 1px border tint, or 9–10px label text. Dark sections are rare: one ink card per screen at most (the "shipped this week" style block, the wordmark card). All small text clears 4.5:1. Captions use #6B6E75, amber text uses #A4501A, never the lamp colour itself.
04

Glow

RECIPE Two stacked radial blooms behind the object: a wide amber one (≈1.4× the object's width, 34px blur) and a small hot core in pale gold (≈0.85×, 20px blur). The object sits on top with a white edge, so the light reads as coming from underneath.
BEHAVIOUR It breathes (6–8s sine, ±8% scale, opacity 0.82–1) and drifts a few pixels toward the cursor. On hover, cards get a small cursor-tracked version of the same lamp at 14% opacity. That's the only hover glow.
WHERE Hero object, featured plan, closing CTA, and the Item page's preview. Nowhere else. If a screen has two lamps, one is wrong.

07

Motion

THE SIGNATURE — LAYER SPLIT
Stacked cards fan apart as you scroll through them, scrubbed to scroll position so they move at your speed. It's the logo, animated. Used once per page.
ENTER Headline words rise out of a clip mask (expo, 1s, 50ms stagger). Support copy rises 16px. Hairlines draw left to right. Sections fade up 28px on entry, once.
RESPOND Cards lift 6–7px with a cursor-tracked lamp. Buttons brighten, press 1px. The glow leans toward the pointer. Marquees slow to a quarter speed under the cursor.
NEVER No bouncing, no auto-playing carousels, no parallax on text, nothing longer than 1.2s. If the user isn't moving, only the lamp breathes.

10

Decisions I need from you

Type: A or B? Figtree + Cormorant pull-quotes, or Figtree alone. Or keep Geist.
Amber stays? The lamp could be pale gold, coral or moss-green instead. Amber is my pick against the graphite mark.
Logo: gradient or flat for the primary? Gradient + grain is closer to your reference; flat ink scales better at 16px. I'd ship both and pick per context.
Real imagery? Tiles are paper tints now. Renders or screenshots of real assets would change the hero a lot — do you have any?

Answer those four and I'll convert the remaining pages and cut the logo files.
