04

Money & the gate

Free tier plus paid unlimited, and you chose all four gating mechanics. They shouldn't all fire at once — each asset type gets the gate that hurts least and teases most.

PROMPTS + TEMPLATES
Full preview, prompt redacted. You see the whole result and the first two lines of the prompt; the rest is blurred with a real character count showing.
3D + BACKGROUNDS
Live demo, unlocked and clickable. Interaction is the sell. Download and source are what you pay for.
IMAGES + VIDEO
Watermarked at low res. Clean file on unlock. Never blur these — buyers judge them visually.
FREE TIER
A genuinely good handful, fully unlocked, refreshed monthly. The free items must be good enough to embarrass the competition's paid ones.
Pricing shape I'd propose: Free · Monthly · Lifetime, with lifetime priced at ~10× monthly and framed as founding-member. Since you're the sole supplier, "creator upload" and "creator earnings" become your studio admin — same screens, private, and they move to the end of the build.

The design system

Cinematic dark stays the default — that's the getlayers register you picked. From your reference boards I'm taking the warm near-black (not blue-black), the cream ink, the green-to-lime accent ladder, and the serif-display-over-neutral-UI type pairing. The same tokens invert into a cream light mode, so marketing pages can breathe while the library stays dark.

One ladder, three jobs: forest for filled buttons and unlocked states on light, lime for the same on dark, olive for hover and active rules. Lime bright is text-on-dark only, where #A7A401 goes too dim. Nothing else gets colour — no per-category hues, no gradient backgrounds.
Dark is home
Library grid, item detail, paywall, dashboard, checkout. Previews are the only bright thing on screen, which is exactly the getlayers effect.
Light is for reading
Pricing, docs, about, onboarding, emails. Cream paper with brown ink and forest buttons — the switch itself signals "this is the studio talking", not the vault.

Card anatomy
UNLIMITED preview loop
Item name 3D SCENE · THREE
Preview fills the frame edge to edge. Access badge top-left, type + stack bottom-right in mono. Title in Instrument Serif. No descriptions on cards, no author avatars, no hover-reveal buttons — the preview is the pitch.
Grid
12-column, 1440 max, 24px gutters. Cards in a 3-up masonry so tall 3D scenes and wide landing pages both sit right. 8px spacing scale throughout.
Form
Radius 12px on cards, 99px on pills, 0 on nothing. Borders are 1px hairlines, never shadows — shadows on near-black just look muddy.
Motion
Previews autoplay muted and loop, staggered so the grid never pulses in unison. Hover lifts the border to lime in 160ms. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no entrance animation on the grid.
