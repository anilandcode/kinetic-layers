05

Rules

Grid
12 columns, 1360 max, 24px gutters. Library is a 3-up masonry so tall scenes and wide pages both sit right. 8px spacing scale.
Form
14px radius on cards, 10px on buttons, 99px on pills. 1px hairlines only — no shadows on dark, one soft shadow allowed on light.
Motion
Previews loop muted and staggered so the grid never pulses in unison. 160ms border and colour transitions. No parallax, no scroll-jacking.
Never
Gradient backgrounds, glass blur panels, purple, emoji, stock 3D blobs. Those are the four tells of an AI template and they cost you the price premium.
