Design system v2 VIOLET / BENTO / PATTERNED

The violet system.

Near-black ground with a violet cast, one drifting bloom per section, and a texture on every surface. Sora stays; the sage palette is retired to v1.

07 — MOTION
BLOOM DRIFT

Blooms follow the pointer at 20–60px, 1.2s power3.out, and breathe on a 9–13s sine loop.

CARD TILT

±5° perspective tilt with a 360px violet glow tracking the cursor inside the card.

MASK REVEAL

Headlines rise word by word out of a clip, 45ms stagger, expo.out.

BATCH REVEAL

Bento and grid cells rise 34px in batches of eight, 70ms stagger, once only.

PATTERN PARALLAX

Pattern layers scrub 40–90px against their card as the section passes.

SWEEP

A single light band crosses the ice tile every 7s. Nothing else animates on loop.
